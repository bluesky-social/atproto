Add support for permissioned spaces ([proposal](https://github.com/bluesky-social/proposals/tree/main/0016-permissioned-data)). The PDS hosts its accounts' repos in any space, and acts as the space host for the spaces its accounts are the authority of.

- Space management: `com.atproto.space.createSpace`, `getSpace`, `updateSpace`, `deleteSpace`, and `listSpaces`. A space's `policy` is one of `com.atproto.personalspace.policy` (only the authority), `com.atproto.memberspace.policy` (a member list managed with `com.atproto.memberspace.putMembers`, `removeMembers`, and `listMembers`, with `publicRead`, `publicWrite`, and an optional `allowedApps` list of OAuth client IDs), or `com.atproto.managedspace.policy` (each decision deferred to a `managingApp` through `com.atproto.managedspace.checkAccess`; if the app does not answer within 10 seconds, the request fails with `UpstreamFailure`). With `publishPolicy` or `publishMembers` set, the PDS publishes the configuration as `com.atproto.space.policy` and `com.atproto.memberspace.member` records in the authority's repo in the space. Those collections cannot be written directly. Deleting a space deletes the authority's repo in it and leaves a tombstone. Other members' repos are not touched.
- Records: `createRecord`, `putRecord`, `deleteRecord`, `applyWrites`, `clearRecords`, `getRecord`, `listRecords`, `getBlob`, and `listBlobs`. Space records are validated as public records are and are limited to 1 MiB. Their blobs are uploaded with `com.atproto.repo.uploadBlob`. An account reads only its own repo in a space. Reading other repos takes a space credential or the admin token. Space writes count against the existing `repo-write-hour` and `repo-write-day` rate limits.
- Sync: `getRepo` (a STAR archive), `getLatestCommit`, `listRepoOps`, `listWriters`, and `removeWriter`. Space repo commits are not signed. A syncer requests an HMAC with `Accept-Signature`, keyed by ECDH against the account's `#atproto_kem` key, and the PDS returns it as an RFC 9421 HTTP message signature.
- Credentials: `getDelegationToken` issues a token for the account. The space host exchanges it in `authorizeSpaceSync` for a space credential bound to a P-256 `did:key` and signed with the authority's `#atproto` key. The space host may also require a client attestation, verified against the OAuth client's published JWKS. If the policy allows anonymous reads, a credential can be issued without a delegation token. Credentials are presented with the `Atproto-Space-Credential` authorization scheme and an HTTP message signature. Space hosts can revoke credentials with `notifyCredentialsRevoked`.
- Notifications: after each space write, the PDS sends `notifyRepoUpdate` to the space host, persisting retryable failures in the account-manager database and retrying them with backoff for up to 24 hours. The space host checks the writer against the policy, sequences accepted updates by `spaceRev`, and forwards them once, on a best-effort basis, through `notifySpaceUpdate` to syncers registered with `authorizeSpaceSync`'s `notify` option (each new registration is first confirmed with `confirmRegistration`). `spaceDeleted` and `registrationExpired` notices are retried for at least 72 hours. Requests to syncers, managing apps, OAuth clients, and other space hosts are made with SSRF protection.
- Account migration: `importRepo` imports an account's space repo from its `getRepo` archive. `exportSpaceMeta` and `importSpaceMeta` move a space's policy, member list, and writer set. Imports respect `acceptingImports` and `maxImportSize`. `uploadBlob` makes a blob permanent if a space record already references it.
- Takedowns: space methods enforce takedown and deactivation as `com.atproto.repo.*` and `com.atproto.sync.*` do. A taken-down or deactivated authority issues no credentials and lists no writers. A taken-down account can still export its space data.
- Storage: new account-manager migrations add the notification retry queues, worker leases, and revoked space credentials, and new actor-store migrations add the space tables.
